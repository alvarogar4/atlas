# Business Model Canvas: {Company}

## Customer Segments

## Value Propositions

- **For Segment 1**: {value proposition}

## Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Stage |
|---------|---------|-------|
| {channel} | {awareness/evaluation/purchase/delivery/support} | {current/planned} |

## Customer Relationships

- **Segment 1**: {self-service/personal/automated/community}

## Revenue Streams
How does each segment pay? What are they willing to pay for?

| Stream | Type | Pricing | Segment |
|--------|------|---------|---------|
| {stream} | {subscription/usage/license/etc.} | {pricing model} | {segment} |

## Key Resources

- **Physical**: {resources}
