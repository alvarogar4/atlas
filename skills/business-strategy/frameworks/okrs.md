## OKR Health Check

### Alignment
- [ ] Objectives support company strategy
- [ ] Key Results are truly measurable
- [ ] 70% target = stretch but achievable

### Quality
- [ ] 3-5 Objectives maximum
- [ ] 3-5 Key Results per Objective
- [ ] Outcomes, not tasks
- [ ] No "business as usual" OKRs

### Cadence
- **Set**: Beginning of quarter
- **Check-in**: Weekly (brief) + Monthly (deep)
- **Score**: End of quarter
- **Reflect**: What worked, what didn't

## Scoring Guide

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0.0-0.3 | Failed to make progress |
| 0.4-0.6 | Made progress but fell short |
| 0.7-0.9 | Delivered (sweet spot for stretch goals) |
| 1.0 | Fully achieved (goal may have been too easy) |

**Target average**: 0.6-0.7 across all OKRs
