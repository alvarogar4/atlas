# Jobs-to-be-Done Framework

Best for: Understanding customer needs, innovation

```yaml
---
type: strategy
framework: jtbd
product: {product}
date: {date}
---

# Jobs-to-be-Done: {Product/Customer}

## Job Statement
**When** {situation}
**I want to** {motivation}
**So I can** {expected outcome}

## Job Dimensions

### Functional Job
What task is the customer trying to accomplish?

- **Core job**: {main task}
- **Related jobs**: {adjacent tasks}
- **Emotional jobs**: {how they want to feel}
- **Social jobs**: {how they want to be perceived}

### Circumstance
When and where does this job arise?

- **Trigger**: {what prompts the job}
- **Context**: {situational factors}
- **Constraints**: {limitations}

## Current Solutions

| Solution | Hired For | Fired Because |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| {current solution} | {what job it does} | {why it fails} |

## Outcome Statements
What does success look like?

| Outcome | Importance | Satisfaction | Opportunity |
|---------|------------|--------------|-------------|
| Minimize {unwanted outcome} | High/Med/Low | High/Med/Low | Score |
| Increase {desired outcome} | High/Med/Low | High/Med/Low | Score |

**Opportunity Score** = Importance + (Importance - Satisfaction)
- Score > 10: Underserved (opportunity)
- Score 8-10: Appropriately served
- Score < 8: Overserved

## Progress Making Forces

### Push of Current Situation
{What's pushing them away from status quo}

### Pull of New Solution
{What's attracting them to a new solution}

### Anxiety of New Solution
{What concerns do they have about switching}

### Habit of Current Situation
{What keeps them with current solution}

## Implications for Product/Strategy
{How to design solution based on JTBD insights}
```

## When to Use

- Product development
- Marketing messaging
- Innovation strategy
- Customer research
